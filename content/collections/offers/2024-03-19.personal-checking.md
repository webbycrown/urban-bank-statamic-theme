    body: |-
      - A personal checking file in your name (or joint, if the sheet allows it)
      - Debit on eligible spend, with freeze from the app or the desk
      - Holds on cheques or large credits explained in the same hold language as transfers
      - A monthly note that lists fees, holds, and any bonus as separate lines

    heading: If you are new — the $500
    body: |-
      New personal checking customers may earn up to $500 when the offer is in force. The amount, the qualifying deposits, and the deadline are on the sheet handed to you — not on a banner from last quarter.

      Combined checking-and-savings bonuses are a different offer ([Checking with welcome perks](/offers/checking-perks)). Do not stack headlines. If both could apply, the banker prints one sheet that says which bonus you are in.

      The credit posts after the review date. Closing the account early can reverse it. Returned deposits do not count.
