      **Double UpSM** is an Urban Bank credit card offer. During the promo window printed on the card sheet, eligible spend in named categories earns **double the usual rewards**. Outside those categories, and after the window ends, the card earns the standard rate on the sheet.

      The SM mark is the product name. It is not a second bank. The card is issued by Urban Bank onto the same app as your accounts when you already bank with us, or as a card-only file if the sheet allows it.

    id: du-how
    type: text_section
    heading: How Double Up works
    body: |-
      The sheet lists:

      - The **start and end dates** of the double-rewards window
      - The **categories** (for example groceries or transit — only what the current sheet names)
      - The **cap**, if extra rewards stop after a monthly or campaign maximum
      - The **standard earn rate** after the window

      A purchase posts to the category the network sends us. If a supermarket codes as general merchandise, it may not double. The monthly note shows the earn for that purchase. If you think a ticket was miscoded, send [Talk to us](/contact) with the date and last four. We can only adjust what the network file allows.

      Interest, if you revolve a balance, is the rate on the card face and the sheet. Rewards do not change that rate.
