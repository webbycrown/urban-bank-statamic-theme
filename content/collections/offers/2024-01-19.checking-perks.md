    id: chk-how
    type: text_section
    heading: How the $500 is earned
    body: |-
      The offer sheet names two things: the **deposit amount** and the **deadline**. Direct deposit from payroll counts when the payer is the same name as the account. A cash or cheque deposit counts when it clears — not when it is handed across the desk.

      Typical steps:

      1. Open checking and savings in the same application window
      2. Complete the qualifying deposits before the date printed on the sheet
      3. Leave both accounts open until the bonus posts
      4. Read the monthly note for a line that names this offer

      If a deposit is returned, that amount does not count. If you close either account before the review date, the bonus can be reversed. We tell you that on the sheet before you sign.
