Urban Bank needs a mobile developer who can work close to the device: freeze a card, unlock with biometrics, and keep a hold note beside the amount so nobody has to guess.

You may work in Swift, Kotlin, or both, and you will pair with the React Native team when a flow is shared. The customer record is the same as the branch. Do not invent a second status for the same payment.

#### What you will do

You will own or share native modules that React Native cannot do well enough: camera for ID at a branch hand-off, secure storage for a session, push notes when a transfer posts, and the freeze control for a lost card.

You will reproduce device bugs that only happen on one OS version, write the fix, and tell support what the customer will see after the update. You will keep crash reports below the bar we set before a store release.

You will sit through a release checklist with QA: fee before confirm, hold text, and card freeze. If a line fails, the build does not ship.

#### Who you work with

React Native, backend, QA, and the support lead who reads freeze tickets. Design will give you sizes for the freeze button and the hold card. You do not change the legal wording on those screens without the writer.

#### What you bring

- Shipped native iOS or Android work, ideally with payments, cards, or identity
- Comfort with store review notes and a staged rollout
- Clear written updates when a freeze or a push message fails
- Willingness to test on real devices in the Jakarta office

#### How to apply

Use **Apply now** on this page. Send a CV and a note about a native feature you shipped (freeze, push, biometrics, or a similar control). We will reply if there is a match.
