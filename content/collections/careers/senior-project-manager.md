Urban Bank needs a senior project manager who can hold a launch date and a sentence at the same time. If the site says a transfer is same-day, the app and the branch must say it that week, not the week after.

You will plan work across design, engineering, content, and the desk. You will not sign loans or move money. You will make sure the people who do that have the screens and the notes on time.

#### What you will do

You will own the calendar for product changes: a new card page, a fee line, a hold template, or a career form. You will write the checklist, name the owner of each line, and call out when copy is still lorem or when a figure is still a placeholder.

You will run a short stand-up and a longer review before go-live. Legal and compliance get a pass on pages that mention rates, limits, or under-18 rules. Marketing does not publish until that pass is done.

You will log what slipped and why, without blaming a channel. The next launch uses that log.

#### Who you work with

Engineering leads, the digital marketer, the writer, branch operations, and whoever owns the confirmation screen. You escalate when two teams have two figures for the same fee.

#### What you bring

- Several years running multi-team launches in a regulated or customer-facing product
- Clear written plans, dates, and owners — not a slide deck with no names
- Comfort saying a page is not ready
- Jakarta presence for launch week; planning can be remote

#### How to apply

Use **Apply now** on this page. Send a CV and a one-page example of a launch plan you ran (dates and roles can be anonymised). Tell us one thing that slipped and how you changed the next launch. We will reply if there is a match.
