Urban Bank needs a React Native developer who can keep the iOS and Android apps on the same customer record as the branch. A hold, a fee, and an incoming transfer should read the same on the phone and at the counter.

This is a product role on a banking marketing and app team. It is not an internet-banking dashboard job. You will ship screens customers already see: send money, cards, and the note that explains why a payment waits.

#### What you will do

You will build and maintain the Urban Bank apps in React Native. Day to day that means transfer flows that show the fee before confirm, card screens that list the rate when the network sends it, and hold notes that use the same words as the desk.

You will work from the same designs the web team uses, so a product page and the app do not drift. You will write the small copy with content people when a button or an error has to be plain.

You will test on common Android and iOS sizes, fix crashes that block a send, and sit with backend when a same-day transfer is late.

#### Who you work with

Design, backend, and the product writer who owns fees and holds. Branch staff will send you a note when the app and the counter disagree. You fix the screen, not the legal file.

#### What you bring

- Shipped React Native apps, ideally with forms, lists, and money amounts on screen
- Care for accessibility, loading states, and copy that fits a small phone
- Comfort talking through a hold or a returned payment with a non-engineer
- Jakarta office time for launch weeks; remote days are fine when the build is stable

#### How to apply

Use **Apply now** on this page. Send a CV and a short note about a banking or payments screen you shipped. Say how the fee or the wait was shown before the user confirmed. We will reply if there is a match.
