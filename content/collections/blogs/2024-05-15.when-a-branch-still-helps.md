### Bank customer

Most Urban work is in the app: transfers, card freeze, statements, and fee screens. A branch still helps when we need to see original documents, count cash, or witness a signature. Those jobs are slower on a scan because someone still has to match the paper.

Bring the ID listed in the appointment note. A photo of a passport on a phone is not enough for a notarised instruction.

### What to book

Cash deposits above the in-app limit, replacing a chip-and-PIN card you cannot freeze-and-reorder, and signing a loan that was already approved in the app. For a loan, the branch meeting is the signature, not a new credit check, unless your figures changed.

Safe-deposit and large cash orders need a booking. Walk-in for those is often turned away the same day because the cash is not on the premises.

### You get

A printed receipt with the same reference the app will show later. If the app is delayed, keep the paper. The reference is how we find the job.

### Hours and what we cannot do at the desk

Head office hours are Monday to Friday 9:00–18:00 and Saturday 10:00–14:00. We do not open Sunday. We do not reverse a posted card purchase at the desk; that is a merchant dispute from the app.

Foreign cash is ordered, not kept in the till for every currency. Ask two working days ahead.

If you only need a statement or a fee explanation, stay in the app or write to support. A branch visit will print the same pages you can already download.
