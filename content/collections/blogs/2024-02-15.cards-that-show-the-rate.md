### Bank customer

A card purchase is not finished when the terminal beeps. Urban first places a hold for the authorised amount, then replaces it with the posted amount when the merchant settles. The card screen shows both, so a hotel or car hire hold does not look like a vanished payment.

Foreign purchases show the rate Urban will use at posting time, not a marketing mid-market rate. If the merchant settles in a different currency than the authorisation, the posted amount can move. We keep the original hold visible until that settle arrives.

### What the rate line means

The rate is locked when the purchase posts, not when you tap the card. Until then the screen labels it **indicative**. That word is there on purpose. It is not a guarantee for a restaurant bill that settles three days later.

ATM withdrawals abroad list a separate cash fee on the same row as the rate. In-app card controls can switch foreign use off if you are not travelling.

### You get

A notification when a hold drops off without a matching post — for example an unused hotel authorisation. The money is available again the same day we release it.

### Holds that look like spending

Car hire and hotels often authorise more than the final bill. Urban shows that extra as a hold, not as spent. When the merchant sends the true amount, the hold closes and only the bill remains.

If a merchant never settles, we release the hold on the timetable printed on the card detail. You do not need to phone us for a routine release unless the date has passed and the hold is still there.

Use Cards in the app to freeze a lost card. Freeze does not cancel recurring merchants you already approved; turn those off under Recurring.
